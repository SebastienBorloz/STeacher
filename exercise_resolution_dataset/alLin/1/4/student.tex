\[ \text{Exo 4)} \]
\[ \text{Il faut faire une projection orthogonale} \]
\[ \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \]
\[ \text{Given points (1,1,1) and c(a,0,1), the vector connecting them is:} \]
\[ \Rightarrow \begin{pmatrix} a-1 \\ 0-1 \\ 1-1 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} a-1 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \]
\[ \text{Projection de } \begin{pmatrix} a-1 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \text{ sur } \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}: \frac{\begin{pmatrix} a-1 \\ -1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}}{\begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}} \cdot \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = R \]
\[ \vec{OP} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 1 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} + R \cdot \begin{pmatrix} -2 \\ 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \begin{pmatrix} V_1 \\ V_2 \\ V_3 \end{pmatrix} \Rightarrow P = (V_1, V_2, V_3) \]
